Alloy shackles utilize a bolt and nut with linchpin to secure the assembly. Used on web slings 3"–6" wide. All shackles galvanized for longer life. Marked with WLL and size.

Web sling shackles are designed to connect synthetic web and round slings to other lifting hardware. The wide, flat bearing surface protects the sling from cutting and concentrated wear that a standard shackle bow would cause.

Do not point load — distribute load evenly over the entire pin to achieve full WLL. Cotter or hairpin available on special order.

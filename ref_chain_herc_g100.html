Transport (Binding) Chain — Grade 70
Working Load Limit: 3,150 to 15,800 lbs.
⚠ Do not use for overhead lifting

Grade 70 Transport Chain is a high-carbon steel chain, heat treated and zinc plated with yellow chromate finish for corrosion resistance and easy identification. Designed for cargo securement and binding applications.

Meets ASTM & NACM standards. Meets FMCSA & CVSA requirements. Thermal heat treated using advanced technology for superior wear and toughness. 100% proof tested with 4:1 design factor.

Permanent identification embossed on chain (G70, plus 3-letter trace code). Full weld trim.

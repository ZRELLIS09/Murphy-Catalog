7 x 19 Vinyl Coated
PVC Jacket · SS 304 & 316

7x19 stainless steel vinyl coated cable features a PVC jacket over a flexible 7x19 stainless steel core, providing additional corrosion protection, abrasion resistance, and a smooth, non-marring surface. Available in both Type 304 and Type 316 stainless steel cores.

Commercial grade — NOT intended for aircraft use. Never use at breaking strength; 10:1 safety factor minimum recommended. Do not use for overhead lifting.

5:1 Design Factor. Values for 6x19 IWRC EIPS rope, mechanical splice per ASME B30.9. Basket hitch assumes rope D/d ratio ≥ 25.

Construction: 6x19 IWRC (Independent Wire Rope Core), EIPS (Extra Improved Plow Steel). Mechanical splice — sleeve swaged with calibrated press. Each sling proof tested to 2× WLL with certificate.

Eye options: Standard, hand-tucked, mechanical splice (Flemish), thimbled, or hooked. All sizes tagged with WLL, length, and serial per OSHA 1910.184.

NOT recommended above 400°F. Inspect for broken wires, kinking, crushing, bird-caging, and corrosion before each use.

Choker WLL is 73% of vertical WLL per ASME B30.9. Eye-and-loop slings have a large eye at one end (the loop) and a smaller eye at the other (the load end). For double-wrap choker, derate to 65% of vertical.

Construction: 6x19 IWRC EIPS with mechanical splice (sleeve swaged) on both eyes. Larger eye is sized for the rope to pass through; smaller eye attaches to the hook.

Choker hitches are used to grip loose loads (pipes, beams, logs). Sling is passed under or around load; smaller eye is pulled through larger eye; both eyes are placed over hook. Capacity reduces sharply as choker angle decreases below 120°.

Proof tested 2× WLL with test certificate. Tagged with WLL, length, serial per OSHA 1910.184. Not recommended above 400°F.

Hot Dip Galvanized · Forged · Fatigue Rated · ASME B30.26
Benefits & Features
• End fittings Quenched & Tempered or Normalized; bodies heat treated by normalizing
• Hot Dip galvanized steel
• Recommended for straight or in-line pull only
• Forged jaw ends: bolts & nuts for 1/4" through 5/8"; pins & cotters on 3/4" through 2-3/4"
• Modified UNJ thread on end fittings for improved fatigue properties
• Body has UNC threads · Lock Nuts available for all sizes
• Fatigue Rated · Meets or exceeds ASME B30.26
Note: Not all turnbuckles come with bolt and cotter. Sizes 1/4" through 5/8" are fitted with bolts and nuts. Sizes 3/4" and larger are fitted with cotter and pin only.

Turnbuckles — Eye & Eye
Hot-Dip Galvanized · Working Load Limit: 800 to 75,000 lbs.
⚠ Turnbuckles recommended for straight or in-line pull only

End fittings are Quenched & Tempered or normalized, bodies heat-treated by normalizing. Hot-dip galvanized steel. Modified UNJ thread on end fittings for improved fatigue properties; body has UNC threads.

Forged elongated eyes maximize easy attachment and minimize stress. For sizes 1/4" through 2-1/2", a shackle one size smaller can be reeved through the eye.

Fatigue rated to 20,000 cycles at 1-1/2 times WLL. Meets or exceeds ASME B30.26. Meets Federal Specifications FF-T-791b and ASTM F-1145.

Endless Roundslings
Polyester · Color Coded by Capacity · Vertical, Choker & Basket Hitches

Endless roundslings are continuous loop slings made of load-bearing polyester yarn encased in a protective double-wall cover. Color coding identifies rated capacity at a glance per ASME B30.9.

Can be used in vertical, choker, and basket hitch configurations. Flexible design conforms to irregular loads and minimizes load damage.

Warning: Always protect roundslings from corners, edges, or protrusions. Refer to the Sling Protection section of this catalog to choose the right protection product for your lift.
